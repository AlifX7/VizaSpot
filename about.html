Our Story

A Legacy of Trust in Saudi Arabia

VIZA SPOT was founded with a singular vision: to simplify the intricate landscape of travel, visa processing, and government relations for individuals and businesses operating in Saudi Arabia.

Navigating government portals, managing bulk Iqamas, and organizing seamless corporate or personal travel can be daunting. We saw the need for a reliable, fast, and transparent partner. Today, we are proud to serve a diverse clientele, ranging from families reuniting on visit visas to massive delivery networks like Noon, Jahez, Mrsool, and Amazon relying on us for their onboarding and documentation needs.

Our tagline, "Always With You," is not just a slogan; it's a commitment. From the moment you hand us your documents to the final stamp of approval, we stand by your side.
